About

About us.

A multidisciplinary urban planning studio providing professional consulting on construction, live events, and growing business ventures.

Our integrated practice focuses on healthy, performance-driven design, data-based reporting structures, program scheduling, and Integrated Project Delivery (IPD).

The work starts from a simple observation: natural systems regulate themselves — they distribute stress, recycle waste, and restore balance. We have the unique opportunity to study the properties and behaviors of these mechanisms, and use an urban design perspective to apply this natural intelligence in our built environment.

We look at placemaking through a human lens, inspired by Stephen Kellert's foundational research on biophilic design, to bring that regenerative capacity into the places we shape — considered together at the scale of the landscape, the human experience, and the material detail.

Our mission is to provide a leading portfolio of urban technologies that value open space, welcoming diversity, bountiful sustainability, and historic continuity in the city and coastal fabric.

How we got started

Founding Principal

Angela Chinn was born and raised in California, where her love of cities and coastal places took root at age 14. After 15 years working across hospitality, construction, logistics, and live events, she built Stormi Applied Computation Design Co. — devoted to making healthy, happy, productive environments the standard for how we build.

Research

Understanding the regenerative world around us.

Our natural environment has the energy to regenerate, evolve, and adapt. We can observe the patterns in nature that have these characteristics, and design them into our built environment.

Studio I

Environmental Design / Urban Design Studio

We observe the energy nature uses to restore balance and bring new life to eroded ground, and apply that same intelligence to the built environment.

We start with the site conditions most visible in everyday practice, then move into the deeper ecological and sensory factors conventional design tends to overlook.

Each one maps back to the natural mechanisms and biophilic strategies that can bring it into balance.

LandscapeHuman experienceMaterial detail

Studio II

Business Development Studio

We apply the same systems-thinking approach to organizations, working with leadership to advance the vision.

We map how the business works to optimize sustainability, stability, and profitability, and clarify the metrics that matter most.

Then we build on existing internal capacity, from team structure to succession planning.

Thinking in Systems: A PrimerDonella H. Meadows · 2008FoundationalA primer on systems theory, arguing that understanding behavior over time reveals more than isolated events ever could.
Good to GreatJim Collins · 2001FoundationalDistills five years of research into what separates companies that make the leap from good to great from those that don't.
The Fifth DisciplinePeter M. Senge · 1990FoundationalNames systems thinking as the discipline that ties together personal mastery, shared vision, mental models, and team learning.
